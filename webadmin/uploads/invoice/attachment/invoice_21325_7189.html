Sending a letter or parcel overseas or anywhere in Australia? It's easy to work out how much it will cost with our online postage calculator - perfect if you're selling online, want to compare postage costs, or if you keep stamps on hand.

Simply enter the approximate dimensions and weight, and your destination postcode or country, and you'll get the postage amount in an instant.

Australia Post assesses charges for parcels according to their actual or cubic weight equivalent, whichever is greater. Weights are rounded up to the nearest kilogram in all calculations.
