Read Receipt

Read Receipts are a way for you to track the email to make sure that is reaches the destination it is intended for. The way this works is that when the person receives the email, they are asked to send a receipt back to sender as a proof of that the message has been received.

This technique, however, may not be supported by all email clients (Microsoft Outlook/Outlook Express, Mozilla Thunderbird and Eudora are just some well known ones that support Read Receipts).

Please Note: Requesting a Read Receipt doesn't always mean you will get a receipt back, the person viewing your email always has an option to reject sending the receipt
